# Ground goes below the plan's floor only where something needs it

Every column used to be solid down to one flat `floorY`, at least 64 studs under any bed, so a deep River or Lake meant lowering that slab across the whole map. Now each column stops at `floorY` and goes lower only under ground that needs it: a deep bed or a Cave, keeping `rockUnder` studs of rock below. This extends Deep Rock, which ADR 0007 kept for Caves, to water. New worlds start with `floorY` at 0. On `test-map-x2` this cut the solid cells of one River Chunk by 58%, measured on 2026-11-07. The alternative of a shell that follows the surface everywhere would save more, since mountains are also solid, and was left out until a place's size or memory asks for it. The price is that nothing below a column's lowest ground exists, so digging in play or a later Cave must extend it through the plan.
