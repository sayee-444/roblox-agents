# Above the World Floor the ground decides which parts of a Cave exist

ADR 0002 lets the World Plan override everything, and that left a Cave carved as an open trench when the ground over it moved (#22). So a Cave's route in the plan now says what may exist. Above the World Floor, the ground decides which parts do: a Cave only subtracts, and uncovered parts stay in the plan as Dormant Sections. Below the World Floor, the Cave decides, and Deep Rock appears only around it. The alternatives were to remove an uncovered Cave from the plan, which destroys a route the ground may give back and splits nothing cleanly, and to make the plan stay exact and report the conflict, which leaves the trench and makes each move of a Landform a manual repair. The price is that reading the plan no longer shows what is carved, so state stores the carved sections and commands report them. Designed on 2026-11-06, and not built, in #30.
